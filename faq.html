What is a Carat?
A Carat (Karat in USA & Germany) was originally a unit of mass (weight) based on the Carob seed or bean used by ancient merchants in the Middle East. The Carob seed is from the Carob or locust bean tree.

How should I clean my jewelry at home?
You can clean most jewelry with a mild soap and warm water. Use a soft brush for intricate designs, and dry thoroughly with a soft cloth.
What is the difference between karat and carat?
Karat refers to the purity of gold, with 24 karats being pure gold. Carat, on the other hand, is a unit of weight for gemstones, with 1 carat equal to 0.2 grams.
How do I determine my ring size at home?
You can measure your ring size by wrapping a thin strip of paper around your finger and marking where it overlaps. Measure the length and use a size chart or online tool to find your size.
Can I wear sterling silver jewelry in the shower?
It's not recommended to wear sterling silver in the shower, as exposure to soaps and shampoos can cause tarnishing. Remove your jewelry before bathing.
What is the significance of birthstones?
Birthstones are gemstones associated with each month and are believed to bring good luck and positive qualities to the wearer. They make meaningful and personalized gifts.
How can I prevent my jewelry from tarnishing?
Store your jewelry in a cool, dry place, and avoid exposing it to harsh chemicals like perfumes and lotions. Regularly clean and polish your jewelry to maintain its shine.
Are lab-created gemstones as valuable as natural ones?
Lab-created gemstones can be physically and chemically identical to natural ones but are generally more affordable. The value depends on personal preference and the specific market.
How can I tell if a diamond is real or fake?
Conduct the "fog test" by breathing on the diamond; a real diamond disperses the heat instantly, while fake ones stay foggy. Professional jewelers can also perform tests.
